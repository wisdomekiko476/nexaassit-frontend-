How NexaAssist Works

NexaAssist makes it simple for businesses to connect with Nigerian virtual assistants for reliable remote support.

For Businesses

Find support and get your work moving in a few simple steps.

1

Create an Account

Sign up as a business and create your NexaAssist account.

2

Find an Assistant

Browse assistants by skills and services that match your needs.

3

Post or Discuss Work

Describe your requirements and connect with the assistant you choose.

4

Start Working

Hire your selected assistant and communicate directly through the platform.

For Assistants

Build your profile and discover remote opportunities.

1

Create Your Profile

Sign up and showcase your skills, experience and services.

2

Explore Jobs

Browse available opportunities posted by businesses.

3

Apply

Submit applications for jobs that match your skills.

4

Work Remotely

Communicate with clients, complete assigned work and build your experience.

Built for Simple Remote Collaboration

Everything is designed to keep the experience straightforward.

For Businesses

Find people who can help with everyday business tasks without building a full in-house team.

  • Browse assistant profiles
  • Post jobs
  • Review applications
  • Hire assistants
  • Communicate directly

For Assistants

Create a professional presence and connect with businesses looking for remote support.

  • Create an assistant profile
  • Show your skills
  • Discover available jobs
  • Apply for opportunities
  • Manage hired work

Why NexaAssist?

NexaAssist is designed to make remote collaboration easier by bringing businesses and Nigerian virtual assistants together in one place. Profiles, jobs, applications, hiring and communication can all be managed through the platform.
