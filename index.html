FAQ

Questions people may have about NexaAssist.

Here are some of the common questions a new customer or assistant may have.

What is NexaAssist?

NexaAssist is a marketplace designed to connect businesses with Nigerian virtual assistants for remote business support.

Who can use NexaAssist?

Businesses can use NexaAssist to find remote assistance, while qualified individuals can create assistant profiles and apply for available opportunities.

What services can assistants provide?

Services can include virtual assistance, administration, customer support, social media assistance, research, data assistance and other online business tasks.

Can a customer post a job?

Yes. Customers with an account can create jobs and receive applications from assistants.

How does hiring work?

A customer can review applications received for a job and select an assistant. Once the assistant is hired, the job becomes assigned and the parties can communicate through the platform.

Are assistants verified?

NexaAssist includes an administration verification system that allows assistant profiles to be reviewed and verified.

How does payment work?

Payment integration is currently the next development stage of the NexaAssist platform. The marketplace workflow is already being developed separately from the payment system.

Is it free to create an account?

Account creation is currently available through the signup system. Any future platform fees or payment arrangements will be clearly communicated before they are introduced.
