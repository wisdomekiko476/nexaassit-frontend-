Project Demonstration & Development Journey

Welcome to the NexaAssist Project

This page provides a step-by-step walkthrough of the NexaAssist platform, from the public website through the customer and assistant workflows and the administration system.

The purpose of this page is to show what has already been built, what is currently working, and where development stopped before payment integration.

Customer Workflow Completed

Customers can use their account to create jobs and manage their hiring process.

The customer workflow includes posting jobs, receiving applications, reviewing applicants and hiring an assistant.

Post a Job
6

Assistant Workflow Completed

Assistants can create their profiles, view available jobs, apply for jobs and see jobs they have been hired for.

Assistant Dashboard Available Jobs
7

Jobs & Applications Completed

Customers can post jobs and assistants can submit applications. Applications are stored in the backend database and can be reviewed by the customer.

The system also tracks whether jobs are open or assigned and whether applications are pending, accepted or rejected.

8

Hiring Completed

A customer can select an assistant from the applications received. Once hired, the job becomes assigned and the selected assistant receives the hire information.

9

Messages & Notifications Completed

Customers and hired assistants can communicate about their jobs. The system also creates notifications for important events such as applications, hiring and messages.

10

Administration & Verification Completed

NexaAssist includes an administration area for managing users, assistants, jobs, applications and hires.

Administrators can also verify assistant profiles.

Open Admin Dashboard
11

Payment Integration Next Stage

This is the current development point.

The core NexaAssist marketplace workflow has been built. The next major stage is connecting customer payments to the hiring process.

Payment will be integrated into the existing system without removing the current signup, jobs, applications, hiring, messaging, notification or administration functionality.
